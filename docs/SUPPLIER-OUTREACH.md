# Supplier Outreach List — Divot Tool Manufacturing (US-first)

Curated from web research July 2026. Send each a copy of `RFQ-TEMPLATE.md` with the product
photos; log replies in `supplier-comparison.csv`. One honest note up front: many "US"
promotional companies are US-based for design/service but produce metal overseas — if a
literal **Made in USA** stamp matters to the brand, ask that question explicitly in the first
email (it's easy to be surprised late).

## Recommended outreach (this week)

Send the RFQ to **six**: Lone Star, Elite Promo, PinMart, Golf Life Metals, Osborne
Coinage, and GS-JJ (price anchor). Ask every one of them the same five questions so quotes
are comparable:

1. Tooling/mold cost to reproduce the attached ichthys tool (one-time)
2. Unit price at 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000
3. MOQ per *new* custom design (this sets the floor for customer custom orders)
4. Sample cost and lead time; production lead time after proof approval
5. Where is production done, and can you certify Made in USA if so?

Log everything in `supplier-comparison.csv`. Order paid samples from the best two before
committing to a batch.
