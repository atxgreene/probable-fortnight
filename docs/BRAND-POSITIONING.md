# Brand Positioning — Aim for Perfection Golf

## One-liner

**Faith-driven golf accessories designed for golfers who want purpose, precision, and personal meaning on the course.**

## Brand feel

Premium · simple · faith-forward without being heavy-handed · golf-native · clean and
trustworthy · giftable · customizable · small-batch / founder-led.

Not: a generic Etsy shop, low-end merch, aggressive sales, or heavy theology. A boutique golf
accessory brand.

## Tagline system

- Primary: **Faith. Focus. Fairways.**
- Support: *Premium golf divot tools designed to remind every player to aim higher — on and off the course.*
- Anchor: “Aim for Perfection” — Matthew 5:48 (used quietly, as a signature, never as a pitch)

## Brand story (canonical copy)

> Aim for Perfection Golf was created for golfers who see the game as more than a scorecard.
> Every round demands patience, discipline, humility, and focus. Our divot tools are designed
> as a small reminder to pursue excellence with purpose — one shot, one round, and one step
> at a time.

## Product naming

Selected: **Signature Ichthys Divot Tool** (alternates considered: Aim for Perfection
Signature Divot Tool, AP Golf Faith Divot Tool, Matthew 5:48 Divot Tool).

## Product copy (canonical)

> A premium divot repair tool built for golfers who carry their faith onto the course.
> Designed with a distinctive ichthys-inspired form and Aim for Perfection detailing, this
> piece is ideal for personal use, gifts, tournaments, church outings, and custom group orders.

Feature bullets: faith-inspired design · distinctive AP Golf detailing · lightweight ·
tournament/gift/outing ready · custom options · bulk by request.

## Color system

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Black | `#050505` | Page background |
| Charcoal | `#111111` | Cards, panels |
| Gold | `#D6A83A` | Primary CTA, accents |
| Warm Gold | `#F0C766` | Hover states, highlights |
| Fairway Green | `#244B35` | Secondary accent, confirmations |
| Ivory | `#F8F4E8` | Body text |
| Muted Gray | `#A8A8A8` | Secondary text |

## Typography

- **Cormorant Garamond** (serif) — headings and brand moments
- **Inter** (sans) — body, UI, forms
- Script is reserved for the logo/wordmark imagery itself; never set UI text in script.

## Voice

Clear, premium, friendly, confident, faith-aligned. Avoid golf clichés, buzzwords,
over-corporate tone, and cheap merch vibes.

## Audience & conversion paths

1. **Individual golfers** → Shop → buy (or request to order)
2. **Event organizers** (tournaments, churches, corporate, weddings) → Custom quote form — the highest-value path; featured in header, hero, sticky mobile CTA, and use-case cards
3. **Bulk/wholesale** → Bulk & Events page → quote
